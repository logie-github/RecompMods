Real-world weather for your location drives Emerald's weather, rain sounds and lightning.

How it works: your approximate location comes from your IP address, and current weather for that spot comes from Open-Meteo, checked every 30 seconds. The game's weather changes to match. Story weather events take priority.

Weather covered: Clear, Partly cloudy, Overcast, Drizzle, Rain, Heavy rain, Thunderstorm, Snow, Hail, Fog, Heat.

Indoors, the rain sound keeps playing muffled, except in basements and caves. Thunderstorms add half-strength lightning indoors.

This mod was heavily coded by AI and debugged by hand.
